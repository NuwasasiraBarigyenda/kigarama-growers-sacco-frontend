Mobile Money Deposit

Send money to the SACCO via MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, then submit the details below. A cashier will verify and credit your savings account.

Submit a Deposit

Your request will show as Pending until a cashier verifies the transaction against the SACCO's mobile money statement and approves it.

How it works

  1. Send money via your MTN or Airtel mobile money menu to the SACCO's mobile money number (ask the front desk if you don't have it).
  2. Copy the confirmation code from the SMS you receive.
  3. Fill in the form and submit it here.
  4. A cashier verifies it and approves — your balance updates automatically.
